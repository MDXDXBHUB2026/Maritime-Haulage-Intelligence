## 📌 Post Draft

🚢 Excited to share my latest portfolio case study: **Maritime Haulage Contract Intelligence (TRUST 2.0)**!

Back in 2015–2016, managing container carrier haulage agreements across hundreds of European inland corridors (rail, road, and barge) was an operational challenge. Translating vendor rate schedules into enterprise logistics formats (such as TRUST) relied on fragile Excel workbooks, complex VLOOKUP tables, and legacy VBA macros.

I have reconstructed and modernized this mission-critical business capability into a full-stack web application built with **React 19, TypeScript, and Node.js**, augmented by **Gemini AI**:

### 🔹 What the System Solves:
1. **Deterministic Rule Engine**: Multi-terminal facility expansion (e.g. Hamburg DEHAM expanding across Burchardkai and Eurogate terminals for 20ft and 40ft equipment tiers).
2. **5-Band Weight Slab Generator**: Automatically generates canonical weight slab child records linked to parent TRUST IDs with zero-rate suppression.
3. **Legacy Enterprise Serialization**: Direct export to legacy-formatted XLSX and CSV with direction-specific column ordering.
4. **AI Intelligence Layer**: Gemini-powered rate schedule extraction, plain-language validation error diagnostics, and rate anomaly detection without compromising calculation integrity.
5. **Zero-Defect Regression Suite**: Automated test runner executing comprehensive deterministic business-rule assertions in under 5ms.

💡 Built with a clean-room synthetic dataset featuring fictional European logistics carriers (NorthSea Haulage GmbH, Euro Inland Logistics BV, etc.).
